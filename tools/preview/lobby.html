Things to try

  1. In Issue #1, tap a size on a panel, then “Add to stash”. Watch for the burst and the counter.
  2. In the stash drawer, change the quantity, then tap “Check out”.
  3. On the Signal Tee, pick White, then S. That combination shows as sold out.
  4. In the Longbox, open “Filter & sort” and pick Hoodie or a price order.
  5. On Pick your issue, tap “Notify me” on the sealed Issue #2.
